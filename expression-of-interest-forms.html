What is an expression of interest?

An expression of interest (EOI) is a way of selling where buyers submit their best offer and terms in writing by a set date, instead of bidding at an auction. The form collects each buyer's offer so the agent and seller can compare them side by side.

Is an EOI form a contract?

No. The form records a buyer's offer and terms. The contract of sale is signed separately once the seller accepts. Check your wording with your principal or solicitor, and we'll build the form to match.

Can we use the same form for every listing?

Yes. We design it once, then set it up for each new campaign with the property address and closing date.

Can it go on our website?

Yes. It can sit on your own website, or have its own link you can share anywhere.
