Questions

Something else? Book a free 15-minute call and ask us anything.

How much work is this for me?

Very little. Fill in the planner, look at your draft and tell us what to change. About two hours, spread across the project.

Can you fix my Wix, Squarespace or WordPress site?

Usually, yes. If starting fresh would be cheaper in the long run, we'll tell you on the first call.

Do I own the website?

Completely. Your domain, design, words and photos are registered in your name. Leave any time and take it all with you.

What if I don't like the first draft?

Tell us what's not working and we'll change it. If the whole direction is wrong, we start again at no extra cost.
