Questions trainers ask

Something else? Book a free 15-minute call, planned around your sessions.

Why not just stick with Linktree?

Linktree is fine for pointing people places. But once someone is ready to pay you, they want to see prices, proof and a way to ask a question. A list of links sends them back to your DMs, which is the problem you're trying to fix.

I only coach online. Is this for me?

Yes, and online coaches usually get the most out of it. Your site explains what's included each week, how check-ins work, and asks about equipment and timezone before you ever reply.

Can clients book or pay straight from the site?

Yes. We link the app you already use, like Trainerize, PT Distinction, Everfit, Calendly, Mindbody or Glofox. If you don't use one yet, we'll help you pick one that suits how you train.

Where do enquiries go?

Straight to your email the moment someone sends the form, with their answers laid out like the example above. Hit reply, or tap their number to call.

I don't have professional photos.

You don't need them. The example on this page uses none. When you're ready, good phone photos in daylight work well, and we'll send you a short list of what to shoot.

What if my prices or class times change?

On the Care Plan, text us and it's updated the same day. Without it, changes are $75 each. Either way, you never have to log into anything.

Do I own it?

Yes. Your domain, your words, your photos and the site itself are in your name. If you ever leave, it all goes with you.
